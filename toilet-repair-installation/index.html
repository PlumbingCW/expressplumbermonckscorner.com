Toilet Repair and Installation in Moncks Corner, SC

When a toilet overflows, lift the tank lid, push the flapper down and close the shutoff valve behind the bowl by turning it clockwise. That stops more water entering the bowl. If water keeps rising, call the 24/7 line and a plumber can be sent to your Moncks Corner address.

Not every toilet problem is an emergency. A tank that runs, a bowl that rocks or a handle that sticks can wait for a planned visit. Use the table below to tell which is which.

Clogged Toilet: What You Can Try and When to Stop

A flange plunger, not a sink cup, is the right tool. Cover the drain opening, press down slowly and pull back sharply for about 15 seconds. If water drains, flush once to confirm. If it does not drain after a few tries, stop and call.

Several toilets and tubs backing up together mean the problem is not the toilet. That points to the main line, and sewer line cleaning is the right visit. Toys, wipes and other foreign objects need a closet auger or removing the toilet.

Leaks at the Base and Failed Wax Rings

A wax ring seals the toilet to the drain flange on the floor. When it fails, water seeps out at the base each flush. The leak may be small, but a slow leak soaks subfloor, and in a humid climate the rot grows fast. Replacing the ring means lifting the toilet, checking the flange and setting the bowl again.

Running Toilets and High Water Bills

A toilet that runs wastes water quietly. Add a few drops of food coloring to the tank. If color appears in the bowl within fifteen minutes without flushing, the flapper leaks. A flapper or fill valve costs little, but the water it wastes shows up on the bill. If your bill has jumped and the toilets are fine, our leak detection service looks elsewhere.

Installing a New Toilet

1

Choose the fit

Rough-in distance, bowl shape and height matter. The plumber measures before you buy.

2

Remove the old toilet

Water is shut off, the tank drained and the bowl lifted away.

3

Check the flange

A damaged flange is repaired or replaced so the new toilet sits level.

4

Set, seal and test

A new wax or rubber seal is used, the bolts snugged evenly and the toilet flushed several times.

Many Moncks Corner homes were built in the last two decades, and their toilets are reaching the age when flappers, fill valves and seals fail. Replacing the toilet also lets you choose a water-saving model with a better flush. For other new fixtures, see plumbing installation.

Codes and Standards Behind This Work

Look for the EPA WaterSense label when you choose a new toilet. WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) governs the flange height and the vent.
