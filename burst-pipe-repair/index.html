Burst Pipe Repair in Moncks Corner, SC: Do This First

A burst pipe is a split or break in a water line that lets pressurized water pour into a wall, ceiling, crawlspace or attic. Stop the water first. Find the main shutoff valve, usually at the meter near the street or where the line enters the house, and turn it fully off. Then call our 24/7 line and a plumber can be sent to your address.

Open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the remaining water. If water is near outlets or a panel, switch off power only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water. Move furniture and electronics away from the spill and take photos for your insurer before you clean up.

Where Pipes Burst in Berkeley County Homes

  • Attics and exterior walls during a rare hard freeze.
  • Crawlspace and garage supply lines with no insulation.
  • Outdoor hose bibs where a hose was left attached.
  • Old galvanized or brittle plastic pipe that fails from age and pressure.
  • A supply line that has been rubbing a stud or joist for years.

Moncks Corner usually has mild winters, but the record low is 11 degrees Fahrenheit. When a rare freeze arrives, many homes have no freeze planning at all. Pipes in unheated garages and attics split first, and the break often shows only after the pipe thaws.

How a Burst Pipe Repair Goes

1

Confirm the water is off

The plumber checks the main valve and the meter to be sure the flow has stopped.

2

Find the full damage

Water travels, so the plumber traces the split and looks for weakness in nearby pipe.

3

Cut out and replace

The damaged section is cut out and new pipe and fittings are installed.

4

Pressure test

The line is turned back on, tested and watched for drips before the wall is closed.

Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized pipe each repair differently. A sleeve or coupling often fixes a single split. A line with several failures may be better served by repiping than by repeated patches.

After the Pipe Is Fixed: Drying and Insurance

Water that soaks drywall and insulation can grow mold in a day or two in this humid climate. Ask the plumber where the water went. Then call a water-damage drying company before the walls are closed. Many homeowner policies cover sudden pipe bursts but not slow leaks, so photos and the plumber's notes help your claim.

If you cannot tell where the water is coming from, our leak detection service pinpoints it without opening more of the wall than needed. If the leak is under a concrete floor, the right page is slab leak repair.

Preventing the Next Burst Pipe

Insulate exposed pipe in the garage, attic and crawlspace, and disconnect hoses from outdoor faucets before a freeze. Know where your main valve is and test that it turns. A valve that is stuck is the wrong thing to discover at midnight. If water pressure runs very high, a failed pressure reducing valve can stress every pipe in the house.
