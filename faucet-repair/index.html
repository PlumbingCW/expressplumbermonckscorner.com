Faucet Repair and Replacement in Moncks Corner, SC

Faucet repair means finding the worn part that causes the drip or the weak flow, then replacing it. Most drips come from a cartridge, ball, washer or seat inside the handle. A faucet with a cracked body or a corroded base is replaced instead.

If water is spraying from a supply line under the sink and you cannot stop it, close the shutoff valves beneath the sink, or shut off the main. Then call our line. Emergencies are answered 24 hours a day, though most faucet work is planned.

Why Faucets Drip, Leak or Run Weak

SymptomLikely causeTypical fix
Drip from the spoutWorn cartridge, washer or seatReplace the worn part
Leak at the handleWorn O-ring or packingReplace seals
Leak under the sinkSupply line or drain connectionNew supply line or fitting
Low or uneven flowMineral buildup in the aeratorClean or replace aerator
Hot and cold swap or mixFailed cartridgeReplace cartridge

Mineral buildup is the usual cause of weak flow. We do not quote a hardness number for Moncks Corner, because it depends on your utility source. Read your utility's water report or use a test strip to see what is in your water.

Kitchen, Bathroom and Shower Faucets

Kitchen faucets work hardest. A pull-down sprayer hose that leaks inside the cabinet, or a handle that loosens from daily use, is common. Bathroom faucets drip more often because the cartridges are smaller. If the shower valve drips or the temperature drifts, the cartridge behind the handle has usually worn out.

Outdoor Faucets and Hose Bibs

An outdoor faucet that drips behind the wall can soak the sheathing. A hose left attached through a cold night can split the pipe inside the wall. Moncks Corner freezes are rare, but the record low is 11 degrees Fahrenheit. A frost-proof hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the house.

Repair or Replace: How to Decide

Repair when

  • The faucet is a few years old
  • Only one part is worn
  • Parts are easy to match
  • The finish is still good

Replace when

  • The body is cracked or corroded
  • Parts are discontinued
  • You want a new style or touchless model
  • Repairs keep recurring

If a new faucet needs a different sink hole layout or new shutoff valves, tell us. We can swap old valves for new quarter-turn ones at the same visit. For a larger remodel, see plumbing installation.
