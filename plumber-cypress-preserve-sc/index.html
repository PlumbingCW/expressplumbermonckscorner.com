A Newer Community Off Cypress Gardens Road

Cypress Preserve is a master-planned community of new single-family homes, built by Lennar, with a sales office at 302 Little Brook Lane in Moncks Corner, ZIP 29461. The builder places it off Cypress Gardens Road near US 52. Its page lists several home collections, and the builder says some phases are still coming, so the community is not yet finished.

The builder lists Foxbank Elementary, Berkeley Middle and Berkeley High as the assigned schools, and names Berkeley County Water and Sewer for water and sewer service. We have not confirmed those details with the school district or the utility, so use your own bill and enrollment papers.

Warranty First, Then a Plumber

With a new house, the first call is often to the builder. Many builders give a written warranty that covers certain plumbing items for a set time, but the terms differ and we do not know yours. Read the document, note how problems must be reported and what is excluded, and ask before hiring anyone for a covered item.

Usually a builder question

  • A leak or drip from work done during construction
  • A fixture that was installed incorrectly
  • A problem that began in the first weeks in the home
  • Anything your warranty document names as covered

Often fine to call a plumber about

  • A clog caused by what went down a drain
  • A fixture you chose and installed yourself
  • An addition such as a filter or extra faucet
  • A problem the warranty excludes or has outlasted

New-Home Checks Worth Doing Early

  • Find the main water shutoff and meter, and turn the valve once to confirm it moves.
  • Rinse each faucet aerator if water sputters, since debris can collect on the screens.
  • Test your tap at every sink and tub for hot water, and tell the builder about any that are slow to warm.
  • Check under each sink for damp spots after a few days of use.
  • Photograph anything wet or dripping with the date, before you call anyone.

The builder lists Dominion Energy as the gas utility. If your home uses gas and you ever smell it, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility from outside before doing anything else.

Cypress Preserve Plumbing Questions

The builder lists Cypress Preserve at 302 Little Brook Lane in Moncks Corner, ZIP 29461, off Cypress Gardens Road near US 52. That address is the sales office, and we have not seen a published boundary for the finished community.

For a new home, read your builder warranty first. Many builders offer a written warranty, and the terms and the reporting steps vary. Using someone else for a covered item can affect a claim, so ask the builder how repairs should be handled before you hire anyone.

The builder's community page lists Berkeley County Water and Sewer for water and sewer, Berkeley Electric Cooperative for electric and Dominion Energy for gas. We have not verified that independently, so confirm the provider on your own bill.

Small bits of debris can reach faucet screens in a new home. Unscrew the aerator, rinse it, and run the water for a minute. If the water is cloudy or the problem returns, call and describe it.

If your home uses gas and you smell it, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility from outside. Do not turn lights or appliances on or off. A plumbing call comes after the area is safe.
