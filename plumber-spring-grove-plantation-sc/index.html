Spring Grove Plantation at a Glance

Spring Grove Plantation is listed in Moncks Corner, ZIP code 29461, off Cypress Gardens Road. Listing sites describe mostly single-family homes, in one-story and two-story layouts, built from the mid 2000s through 2020. Streets named in listings include Maywood Drive and Neosho Court.

The same listings say children are commonly assigned to Foxbank Elementary, Berkeley Middle and Berkeley High, and describe a pool, playgrounds, trails and a dog park. Those details come from real estate descriptions that we could not confirm with the association, so check them before relying on them.

Homes in Their Second Decade

A house built between the mid 2000s and 2020 is now somewhere between about five and twenty years old. Most plumbing from that period was installed well, but small parts wear with use. This is general plumbing information and not a claim about any one home in Spring Grove.

Parts that commonly wear first

  • Toilet flappers and fill valves that keep the tank running
  • Faucet cartridges that drip at the spout
  • Supply hoses under sinks and behind washers
  • Garbage disposal motors and seals

What is worth checking now

  • The water heater label for its manufacture date
  • The shutoff valves under each sink and toilet
  • The main shutoff, so it turns when you need it
  • The meter, with all fixtures off, for slow leaks

Water Heaters, Fixtures and the Water Bill

Tank water heaters commonly last somewhere around eight to twelve years, so tanks installed when a home was new are reaching that range in older parts of the neighborhood. A leak at the base means the tank itself has failed. Noise, short hot showers or a pilot that will not stay lit are different problems that sometimes need only a part.

If your water bill jumps, do a two hour meter test before assuming a leak. Turn off every fixture, read the meter, wait, and read it again. A change points to a hidden leak, and our leak detection page explains how one is located without opening walls.
