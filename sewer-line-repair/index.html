Sewer Line Repair in Moncks Corner, SC, After a Backup

Sewer line repair means fixing the pipe that carries wastewater from your house to the public sewer or septic system, by replacing a broken section, lining it from the inside or replacing it entirely. When sewage backs up into a tub or floor drain, call our 24/7 line and a plumber can be sent to your Moncks Corner address.

Stop running water, keep everyone away from the affected area and leave the toilets unflushed until the plumber arrives. Sewage is a health hazard, so keep pets and children away and do not clean it up with bare hands. After service, your insurer may ask for photos of the backup and the camera video.

What a Cracked or Rooted Sewer Line Looks Like

  • Gurgling fixtures, especially when a toilet flushes or the washer drains.
  • Multiple drains slow at once, not just one sink.
  • Sewage smell in the yard or garage, or green wet spots over the line.
  • Repeated backups after cable cleaning, sometimes within weeks.
  • A camera shows a crack, offset joint, bellied section or dense roots.

A localized crack with root intrusion is one of the most common repair calls, and it is often a good candidate for a spot repair rather than a full replacement. We cannot know that until the camera has been run, so our sewer inspection comes before any repair quote.

Trenchless lining fits a long run with cracks but no collapse. It sets a resin sleeve inside the old pipe. It cannot fix a pipe that is crushed or has a major offset. We will tell you if it is not a fit.

Sewer Lines in Moncks Corner's Wet Ground

Berkeley County soil holds water, and Moncks Corner averages about 52 inches of rain a year. That means pipe sits in damp ground much of the time, which allows roots to enter joints and lets soil shift under a line. The town is also close to the Cooper River at about 33 feet above sea level, so groundwater rises after storms.

In older neighborhoods near downtown, sewer laterals may be clay or cast iron with lead-and-oakum or cement joints. Newer subdivisions use PVC. Different materials fail in different ways, which is why a camera matters more than guessing from the age of the house.

Who Owns the Sewer Line, and Who Do You Call

In most places, the homeowner owns the lateral from the house to the connection at the street or main. Utilities own the main. Public Works in Moncks Corner and Berkeley County Water and Sanitation handle their own systems, so a blockage in the street main is a call to the utility, not a plumber. Our camera work shows which side of the line the problem is on.

If the problem is only a clog and the pipe is sound, sewer line cleaning is the right visit. When the pipe wall itself is failing, repair is the fix. For repeating clogs without breaks, consider hydro jetting.
