Plumbing in Goose Creek, SC: What Makes It Different

Goose Creek is Berkeley County's largest city, with 45,946 residents at the 2020 Census, and it sits about 13 feet above sea level between the Cooper River and Back River. That low elevation matters. Heavy summer storms can leave yards and street drains full for hours, and sewer laterals near the river side of town work harder after long rains.

The housing stock splits into three eras, according to the city's comprehensive plan. About 23 percent of homes date to the 1970s or earlier, 19 percent to the 1980s and 18 percent to the 1990s, while 41 percent were built between 2000 and 2018. Each era needs a different plumbing conversation, and we start every Goose Creek call by asking when your home was built.

Older Goose Creek Homes: Pipes From the 1970s and Earlier

Homes from the 1970s and before, including those around Pineview Hills and the older streets off St. James Avenue, may still have galvanized steel supply lines or original cast iron drains. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside, so water pressure drops over the years even when nothing leaks. Cast iron drains crack and scale, which catches paper and grease.

If a bathroom fills slowly or the kitchen sink gurgles when the washing machine drains, a drain cleaning visit is the first step. A camera run then shows whether the pipe is still sound. When the interior is worn, a whole-house repipe replaces the supply side in one project instead of leak by leak.

Newer Subdivisions: Water Heaters and Fixtures at Their Limit

More than 40 percent of Goose Creek's homes were built between 2000 and 2018, which puts original water heaters, toilets and faucets in the age range where parts fail. Neighborhoods such as Crowfield Plantation, Marrington Plantation and Hounds Run were built in that period, and builders of the time often installed a basic tank unit sized for the original family.

When a tank of that age begins to leak at the base, repair is not an option and water heater replacement is the fix. If the tank is only losing heat or popping, a water heater repair visit may restore it. Either way the plumber tells you which, and you hear the price first.

What the City and County Run, and What You Own

The City of Goose Creek published a service line inventory in November 2024 and reported that it had visually inspected over 78 percent of the service lines, sampled the rest and found no evidence of lead service lines in its system. The city also reminds residents that the connection between the meter and the house is separate from city control, so check your own connection.

Public Works is at 519 North Goose Creek Boulevard, and its water division publishes a Consumer Confidence Report. We do not quote hardness numbers from memory. Read the city's report or test your tap before choosing a softener, and ask us how mineral buildup affects your tank.

Landmarks and Roads We Know

We dispatch into Goose Creek from I-26 exits 199, 203 and 205, from Goose Creek Boulevard, and from Red Bank Road, which connects Marrington Plantation to the rest of the city. Most of Naval Support Activity Charleston lies inside the city, and Joint Base Charleston families rent and buy across the area. Frequent moves mean frequent water heater swaps and fixture upgrades.

Historic sites such as the Otranto Plantation Indigo Vats and St. James Church sit among modern subdivisions, and older properties near them often have mixed plumbing of different ages. If you cannot tell what material your pipes are, tell the dispatcher and the plumber will check on arrival.
