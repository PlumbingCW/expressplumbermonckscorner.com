Downtown Moncks Corner, SC

Moncks Corner is the Berkeley County seat, and the town center grew around its railroad. The Northeastern Railroad laid tracks in 1856, and the depot became the center of the new town. The depot was renovated in 2000 and now serves as a visitor and cultural center. Town Hall is at 118 Carolina Avenue, ZIP 29461.

Main Street is the retail spine. A clothing store called Barron's Department Store opened there in 1923, and a redevelopment association called Corner Renaissance works on downtown. The Town does not publish a downtown boundary that we found, so we use these places as anchors and make no boundary claim.

Older Mixed-Use Buildings: What to Ask

Downtown blocks usually mix shops, offices and civic buildings of different ages and owners. That is a general description, and we have not verified the age or condition of any specific building. When a problem appears, the first question is whether the pipe belongs to the tenant or the property owner.

Tenant or owner

Read the lease. It usually states who handles drains, water heaters and fixtures inside the unit.

Shared walls

Neighboring units can share a drain stack or a water supply, so a problem may start next door.

Permits

Ask the Moncks Corner building department whether the work needs a permit before it begins.

Records

Keep dates, photos and invoices. A landlord or insurer will ask for them.

Restaurants, Offices and Retail

Food businesses depend on drains and grease handling. Ask the sewer utility and the building department whether a grease trap or interceptor is required and how often it must be serviced. Offices and shops have lighter loads, but floor drains, restroom fixtures and water heaters still need attention. Our commercial plumbing page lists the kinds of jobs.
