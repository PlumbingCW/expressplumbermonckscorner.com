Sewer Pipe Line Repair in Moncks Corner, SC: Options Explained

A damaged sewer pipe does not heal. Roots grow, cracks widen and sags hold more waste. The question is how to fix it with the least disruption.

This guide compares your options and what to ask before you sign anything.

Start With the Camera

No one should quote a sewer repair without seeing the pipe. A camera shows where the damage is, how long the damaged section is and what kind of damage it is. Without that, a quote is a guess.

Ask for the recording. It gives you something to show other plumbers, your insurer or a future buyer. Our sewer inspection service includes the video and the distance to each problem.

A line can have more than one problem. A sag may also have a root-filled joint at its low point.

Option One: Spot Repair

A spot repair digs down to the damaged section, cuts out the bad pipe and replaces it. It suits a single crack, offset or short collapse in a pipe that is otherwise sound. It costs less than a full replacement and takes less time, though it leaves a trench to restore.

Ask how deep the pipe is and what lies on top, such as a driveway, patio or tree roots. Those factors decide the cost more than the pipe itself.

Option Two: Trenchless Lining

Pipe lining sets a resin-saturated sleeve inside the old pipe, then cures it to form a new pipe within the old. It suits a run with several cracks and joint leaks where the pipe still holds its shape. Digging is limited to small access points.

Lining cannot fix a collapsed pipe or a major offset. It slightly reduces the pipe's inside diameter, though the smooth new surface usually flows well. A camera check after the cure confirms the result.

Option Three: Full Replacement

Replacement removes the old pipe and installs new PVC from the house to the connection. It is the right answer for a collapsed, severely offset or heavily corroded line. It is the most disruptive and gives the longest-lasting result.

Some replacements use pipe bursting, which pulls new pipe through the old path while breaking the old one. It needs access pits and suits some soil and pipe types.

Choose repair when

  • Damage is in one place
  • The rest of the pipe is sound
  • The line is shallow
  • You want the lowest scope

Choose lining or replacement when

  • Damage runs along the line
  • Repeated failures have occurred
  • The surface is expensive to dig
  • You want to stop worrying about the line

Moncks Corner Notes

Berkeley County ground stays wet, with about 52 inches of rain a year in Moncks Corner, and the town sits only about 33 feet above sea level. Wet soil softens under a pipe and helps roots find joints. Trench plans in soft ground need shoring and pumping, which affects the schedule.

Find out which utility serves your street before assuming a problem is on your side. Public Works in Moncks Corner and Berkeley County Water and Sanitation each run parts of the system. A camera and a distance measurement show whether the damage lies on your lateral or in the street.

Who Owns the Line

In most places the homeowner owns the pipe from the house to the point where it connects to the main. The utility owns the main. If a camera shows damage past your property line, tell the utility. If it is on your side, the repair is yours.

Questions to Ask Before You Hire

  • Will you show me the camera video before quoting?
  • Which repair methods fit my pipe, and why?
  • What does the price include, such as permits and restoring the surface?
  • How long is the work, and how long is the water off or the toilet unusable?
  • What happens if you find something unexpected?

Insurance and Permits

Many policies exclude sewer failure from wear but cover a sudden backup or offer an endorsement. Ask your agent before work starts. Ask the Town of Moncks Corner Building Department whether the repair needs a permit, because rules vary.

How to Compare Quotes

Ask each plumber to put the scope in writing. A good quote names the method, the length of pipe to be replaced or lined, who handles permits, how the surface will be restored and what happens if conditions differ. Two quotes with different prices may cover different work.

Be wary of any quote that skips the camera or promises a method before seeing the pipe. The camera is cheap compared with the repair, and it protects you from paying for the wrong fix.

Warning Signs After Repair

After a repair, run water through every fixture and flush each toilet. Watch the cleanout for several days. Gurgling or a sewage smell after repair means the work needs another look. Keep the before and after videos with your records.

Why Early Repair Costs Less

A cracked pipe lets soil wash into the line and leaves voids around it. The longer it stays that way, the bigger the void and the harder the fix. Roots also thicken with time. A repair done at the first sign of repeated backups is usually smaller than one done after the pipe has collapsed and the yard has sunk.

Standards and Sources

Sewer repair is measured against the International Plumbing Code (IPC) for slope and cleanouts, and trenching begins with a call to 811. Ask your building department which edition applies.
