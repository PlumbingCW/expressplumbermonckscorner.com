Water Heater Repair in Moncks Corner: Start With Why It Failed

Water heater repair means finding the failed part, such as a heating element, thermostat, gas valve or relief valve, and replacing it without swapping the whole tank. When you lose hot water in Moncks Corner at night, our emergency line is open 24 hours a day and a plumber can be assigned to your address.

Before you call, check the breaker for an electric unit or the pilot window for a gas unit. If you see water at the base of the tank, shut off the cold-water valve above it and turn the unit to its lowest setting. A leaking tank is a replacement job, but a leaking fitting often is not.

No Hot Water: What Is Usually Wrong

Electric tanks lose hot water when an upper or lower heating element burns out, a thermostat fails or the high-limit reset trips. A cold shower with the lights still on rules out the breaker and points to one of those parts. Gas tanks fail when the pilot or thermocouple stops sensing a flame, or when the gas control valve fails.

Lukewarm water is a different symptom. It points to a single burned-out element on an electric tank or a dip tube that has broken down inside a newer one. We check temperature at the tap, at the tank and at the relief valve to separate those causes.

Leaking Water Heater: Fixture, Valve or Tank

A drip at the top of the tank usually comes from a supply fitting, a flex connector or the temperature and pressure relief valve. Those are small repairs. Water seeping from the bottom of the tank or the jacket is different, because it means the steel tank has corroded through and no part will fix it.

In a garage, the first sign can be a wet spot on the slab beside the heater, not a puddle under it. We trace the water to its true source before recommending anything. If the leak is in the supply line, a leak detection visit may find it. If the tank is the culprit, water heater replacement is the next step.

Why Water Heaters in Berkeley County Fail Early

Moncks Corner water is supplied by the Moncks Corner Public Works Commission, which publishes an annual water quality report. Mineral content varies by source, so we do not quote a hardness number from memory. Read your utility's report or test your tap, because hard water leaves scale on elements and the tank floor and shortens a heater's life.

Garages in this part of South Carolina run hot in summer and humid year-round, and tank bottoms sit on cool slab concrete. That combination speeds up corrosion on steel tanks. An anode rod, the sacrificial metal rod inside the tank, protects the steel until it is used up, and checking it is a cheap way to add years to a unit.

Gas Safety on Water Heater Calls

If you smell gas near a water heater, leave the house, do not touch light switches and call the gas utility or 911 first. After the utility clears the home, call us to inspect the heater. Any gas appliance repair we do includes checking the vent, the gas connection and the flame.

Tank, Hybrid and Gas Models Compared

Repair makes sense when

  • The tank is under about ten years old
  • The leak is at a fitting or valve
  • A single element, thermostat or sensor failed
  • The unit has been serviced regularly

Replacement makes sense when

  • The tank itself is leaking
  • The unit is past ten to twelve years
  • Repairs are repeating
  • You want a hybrid or heat pump unit for lower energy use

Electric heat pump and hybrid heaters have their own failure points, such as compressor and fan issues. We repair standard electric, gas and hybrid models, and we tell you straight if parts cost more than the unit is worth.

No Hot Water Tonight? Steps Before the Plumber Arrives

  • Check the breaker for electric units and reset it once.
  • Look for the pilot flame on gas units and read the label if you want to try relighting it.
  • Turn off the cold-water supply if you see leaking.
  • Lower the thermostat on a leaking unit and keep children away from the relief valve discharge pipe.
