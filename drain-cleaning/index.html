Drain Cleaning in Moncks Corner, SC, Day or Night

Drain cleaning is the removal of a blockage from a sink, tub, floor drain or main sewer line so wastewater flows freely again. When a drain stops in Moncks Corner at 10 p.m., our emergency line is open 24 hours a day, and a licensed and insured plumber can be sent to your address.

While you wait, stop running water in the house, keep the toilets unflushed and pull any rugs away from the lowest drain. Do not pour chemical drain opener into a standing blockage, because it sits on top of the clog and burns the next person who opens the trap.

When a Clogged Drain Becomes an Emergency

  • Two or more fixtures back up at once, which points to the main line instead of a single trap.
  • Water or sewage rises in a tub or shower when the toilet is flushed or the washer drains.
  • A floor drain in the garage or laundry room overflows or smells like sewage.
  • Wastewater is coming out of the cleanout in the yard.
  • The kitchen sink is full of standing water and the disposal hums without draining.

Any item on that list means the blockage is past the fixture, and the water has nowhere to go but the house. These are the calls that go first in our queue.

Kitchen Sink Clogs and Slow Drains

Kitchen lines clog more often than any other drain we see. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and coats the walls, then food scraps catch on the coating until only a trickle gets through. A garbage disposal that is sluggish usually has the same buildup downstream, not a motor problem.

If the kitchen sink backs up when the dishwasher runs, the shared line is the culprit. We cable the line past the disposal, flush it with hot water and run the dishwasher to confirm the fix. If the disposal itself is leaking or jammed, our garbage disposal repair page explains the next step.

Snaking, Cabling or Jetting: Which Tool Fits

Plumbers use "snaking" and "cabling" for the same family of tools, a rotating steel cable that bores through or hooks a blockage. The right size depends on the pipe. A bathroom sink takes a small hand-fed cable, while a 3 or 4 inch main line needs a drum machine with a heavier cable and a cutting head.

Why Drains Clog Around Moncks Corner

Moncks Corner averages roughly 52 inches of rain a year, and the town sits only about 33 feet above sea level beside the West Branch of the Cooper River. Soggy ground puts weight on buried pipe and lets roots find joints, so a main line that was fine in a dry week can slow after a week of storms.

Homes near Main Street often have decades-old drain pipe with rough interiors, while newer subdivisions use smooth PVC that clogs mostly from what goes down the drain. If you cannot tell which you have, ask the plumber to run a camera through the line. Our sewer inspection service shows the pipe condition on video.

One extra cause turns up in homes with landscaping close to the foundation. A sagging section, called a belly, holds a pool of water and waste. Cables push the blockage through, but the belly refills and the clog returns. That is a repair problem, not a cleaning problem, and our sewer line repair page explains the options.

Drain Line Leaks and Other Problems Found While Cleaning

A drain line that leaks into the slab or crawlspace does not look like a clog at first. You see a damp spot, smell sewage, or find a floor that stays wet while the fixtures drain. If a cable runs freely and symptoms continue, the plumber will recommend a camera and may involve leak detection to find the source.
