Moncks Corner, Our Home Zone

Moncks Corner is the Berkeley County seat and sits south of Lake Moultrie, with its eastern edge reaching the West Branch of the Cooper River. Calls here come from three kinds of property: older downtown homes near Main Street, newer subdivisions built during the town's recent growth, and commercial buildings along US 52 and Drive In Lane. ZIP code 29461 covers the town.

The rest of the town's character matters to a plumber too. Cypress Gardens sits just outside town in swampy terrain, Old Santee Canal Park runs boardwalks over the Biggin Creek backwaters, and the ground in much of the area stays wet after heavy rain. Wet ground can be hard on drains and buried lines, so a soggy yard or a slow drain is worth having looked at.

Goose Creek and the US 52 Corridor

Goose Creek is Berkeley County's largest city and has a dedicated page because its housing and water system differ from Moncks Corner's. Read the emergency plumber Goose Creek page for neighborhoods, ZIP codes and the local water details we verified. Its ZIP code is 29445, and the nearby ZIPs we also serve are 29456 (Ladson), 29410 (Hanahan) and 29406 (North Charleston).

Neighborhoods Inside Moncks Corner

Four areas have their own pages: Foxbank Plantation at US 52 and Cypress Gardens Road, Spring Grove Plantation, the newer Cypress Preserve, and the shops and offices of downtown Moncks Corner. Each page states what we could verify and what we could not.

Smaller Towns We Also Serve

  • Cross, ZIP 29436. A small community in northern Berkeley County, reached from Moncks Corner by road. Rural properties can have long supply lines, and some use well pumps.
  • Bonneau, ZIP 29431. East of Moncks Corner toward the lake country. Water heaters and outdoor faucets are typical rural-property plumbing items.
  • Pineville, ZIP 29468. A small place in the upper part of the county. We handle emergency leaks and drain stoppages here when dispatch can reach you.
  • Huger, ZIP 29450. A community along the Cooper River side of the county, with older and spread-out properties.
  • Summerville, ZIPs 29483 and 29486. A larger town south of Goose Creek, covered for repairs and water heater work. Ask about the address when you call.

How Dispatch Decides Who Goes First

Every call is sorted by what is happening. Active flooding, sewage backups and gas appliance problems go first. A leaking fitting that is under control comes next, and planned work such as a fixture swap is booked into the day. This order is the same whether you live in town or in one of the smaller communities.

When you call, give the street address and the nearest cross street, because several roads in the area share names. If your address is in a place we do not list, call anyway. We will tell you right away whether we can reach you. The number is (855) 575-2890, and it is answered 24 hours a day.

Water and Sewer Providers by Area

Water and sewer service differs by address. The Moncks Corner Public Works Commission and Berkeley County Water and Sanitation each serve parts of the area, and the City of Goose Creek runs its own water division. Your utility owns the meter and the main in the street. You own the pipe from the meter to the house and the drain from the house to the connection.

That split matters on a leak call. A camera and a meter test tell the plumber which side of the line the problem is on, and you then know whether to call us or the utility. Local rules follow the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted by each jurisdiction. Read about the steps on our leak detection and sewer inspection pages.
