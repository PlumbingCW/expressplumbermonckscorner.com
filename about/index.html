What This Business Is

Moncks Corner Express Plumbing is a 24/7 plumbing dispatch line for Moncks Corner and the nearby Berkeley County towns. When you call (855) 575-2890, you reach a person who asks what is happening, helps you shut off water if needed and sends a licensed and insured plumber to your address.

The people who answer the phone are not guessing at your problem. Each call is matched to the type of work involved, so a stopped-up main line goes to someone with drain equipment and a failed water heater goes to someone who carries replacement parts.

What We Promise and What We Do Not

We promise a clear price before work starts, and we promise to tell you if the job changes while the plumber is on site. We offer same-day appointments when a plumber is available, and urgent leaks and backups go first.

We do not publish invented review counts, years in business or awards, because they would not be true. We do not give a time that a plumber will arrive, because traffic, weather and the job before yours all move it. The plumber on your job can tell you a realistic window when you call.

How a Call Becomes a Visit

The person on the phone writes down your address, the symptom and anything you have already tried. That description decides which plumber is sent and what goes on the truck. A leaking tank needs shutoff valves and a replacement plan. A backed-up main needs a large cable machine and sometimes a camera.

While a plumber is assigned, the dispatcher can talk you through the first steps. For a burst line, that means finding the main valve. For an overflowing toilet, it means the small valve behind the bowl. These steps limit damage before anyone arrives, and they are the same ones laid out on our burst pipe repair and toilet repair pages.

The Work We Dispatch For

Most calls fall into three groups. The first is water where it should not be, such as burst pipes, slab leaks and a spinning meter, covered under leak detection. The second is water that will not leave, from a slow sink to a main line backup, covered under drain cleaning. The third is water that is not hot, handled through water heater repair and replacement.

Planned work is welcome too. Fixture swaps, a new disposal, a repipe or a bathroom addition are scheduled around your week, and you hear a clear price before the job is booked. Restaurants, shops and property managers can call the same number for commercial plumbing.

Why the Local Conditions Matter

Moncks Corner sits only about 33 feet above sea level beside the West Branch of the Cooper River, and the area gets roughly 52 inches of rain a year. Wet ground puts weight on buried pipe and lets roots find joints. Humid garages age water heaters faster. A rare hard freeze, with a record low of 11 degrees, catches homes that never planned for it.

The town also grew fast. Officials expected the 2020 Census to show about 60 percent growth since 2010, so many homes are now at the age when original tanks, flappers and supply lines start to fail. Our guides in the plumbing blog explain these local patterns in plain language.

If you are weighing a bigger project, such as a repipe or a water heater installation, the first call is a conversation, not a commitment. Describe the house, ask what the plumber would look at first and ask how the price is built. A plumber who explains the steps in plain words is easier to trust than one who only names a number.

How to Check Any Plumber

Plumbing work is measured against the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted and amended locally, and the Town of Moncks Corner Building Department is the authority inside town limits.

South Carolina residential plumbing contractors are licensed through the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation. You can look up any license yourself at verify.llronline.com, or contact the Residential Builders Commission at Contact.RBC@llr.sc.gov. Ask to see the license, compare names and look for the plumber's insurance too. Do this with every company you hire, including us.
