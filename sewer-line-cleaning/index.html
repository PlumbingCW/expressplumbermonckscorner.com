Sewer Line Cleaning in Moncks Corner When the Main Is Clogged

Sewer line cleaning clears the main drain that carries all household waste out to the street connection or septic tank. A clogged main means toilets, tubs and sinks back up at once. If it happens at night in Moncks Corner, call our 24/7 line and a plumber can be sent to your address.

Stop all water use, do not flush toilets and keep the washing machine and dishwasher off. Water from upstairs fixtures usually surfaces at the lowest drain, which is a basement or ground-level shower or floor drain. Put a bucket or towels there to buy time.

Signs of a Main Line Blockage

  • Several fixtures back up together, not just one.
  • A toilet flush makes the tub or shower gurgle or bubble.
  • Raw water rises out of a floor drain or the cleanout.
  • The sewer smell is stronger outdoors near the cleanout.
  • Water drains slowly everywhere in the house.

The cleanout is a capped pipe in the yard or against the house, often with a white or black cap. Do not open it if water is rising, because pressure behind it can push sewage out. Tell the dispatcher where it is.

Why Mains Clog in Berkeley County

Heavy rain and high water tables let groundwater and roots into laterals through joints. Berkeley County Water and Sanitation notes that several of its systems feed into the county network, and the Town of Moncks Corner runs its own sewer service in town. If the blockage sits beyond your property line, the utility needs to know. A camera and a distance measurement help us tell.

The town averages roughly 52 inches of rain a year, which keeps soil damp. Wet-weather backups often show up one or two days after a storm as the sewer system loads. If your backup follows rain, tell the plumber, because it may be groundwater infiltration into the municipal system and not your line.

Preventing the Next Main Line Clog

Wipes marked flushable do not break down like toilet paper, and grease poured down the sink cools into plugs. Avoid both. Keep the cleanout accessible, and mark it. If roots are the cause, a regular cleaning schedule, or a repair of the entry point, stops the cycle.

Codes and Standards Behind This Work

The International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires cleanout access on building sewers, which is what makes main line cleaning possible without pulling a toilet. If yours is buried, call 811 before anyone digs it up.
